# ADR-002 · Commercial Agent Services Site 2026

Date: 2026-08-30
Status: Proposed in `feat/agent-services-2026`

## Context

The existing homepage is primarily a public/legal reference. Jadel Tech RD now has a broader portfolio of agentic, media, analytics, integration, governance, revenue-research and quantitative-research repositories. The website needs to explain those capabilities without representing experimental or gated systems as production-ready.

## Decision

1. Keep GitHub Pages as the static origin and Cloudflare as the public DNS/TLS edge.
2. Preserve the existing public legal routes: `?view=privacy`, `?view=terms`, and `?view=data-deletion`.
3. Convert only the home experience into a commercial Agentic Systems Studio.
4. Use semantic HTML generated by dependency-free JavaScript plus CSS; no frontend framework or third-party runtime dependency is introduced.
5. Productize capabilities into a visible catalog with three maturity labels: service/implementable, supervised pilot, and research-only.
6. Add service filters, an interactive scope configurator, transparent launch pricing, methodology, governance controls, FAQ and structured data.
7. Never publish fabricated customers, logos, ROI, uptime, production status, profitability or autonomous capabilities.
8. Keep sensitive/external actions behind least-privilege permissions, deterministic policy gates and human approval when appropriate.

## Market benchmark

Reviewed current public pricing and product-positioning patterns from Lindy, Relevance AI, Sintra, Artisan, Beam AI, SmythOS, Stack AI, Salesforce Agentforce and modern 2026 SaaS design references from Webflow/Framer.

Observed market patterns:
- low-friction SaaS subscriptions roughly in the tens of USD per month;
- scale/team tiers in the hundreds of USD per month;
- usage/action/conversation pricing for variable workloads;
- managed agent implementation and enterprise programs ranging from four to five figures;
- high-converting pages make the product visible, explain outcomes, expose pricing or pricing logic, use interactive demos/configurators, and establish trust before the final CTA.

## Launch pricing policy

Prices on the preview are commercial estimates, not competitor prices or guaranteed quotes. Final scope is expected to vary roughly ±25% based on integrations, volume, security/compliance and operating requirements. Third-party model/API/SaaS usage, taxes and extraordinary compliance work are excluded unless explicitly quoted.

Top-level launch anchors:
- Agentic Blueprint: from US$250 one-time.
- Business Agent: from US$1,500 setup.
- Agentic System: from US$4,500 setup.
- Managed Agent Platform: custom, from US$10,000.

## Consequences

Positive:
- substantially stronger commercial storytelling without changing hosting architecture;
- zero additional browser runtime dependencies;
- explicit maturity labeling reduces misleading claims;
- legal/compliance routes remain stable;
- easy rollback because production is unchanged until PR merge.

Known gaps / follow-up:
- the source HTML title/description is still legal-oriented before JavaScript executes; update server-visible SEO metadata in a follow-up before broad acquisition campaigns;
- no public lead form is added until a real, verified destination and privacy workflow exist;
- a future contact backend should use a Cloudflare Worker, Turnstile, rate limiting and server-side secret storage rather than client-side API keys;
- add automated Lighthouse/axe checks and privacy-safe conversion analytics before paid acquisition.
