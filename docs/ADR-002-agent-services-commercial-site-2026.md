# ADR-002 · Commercial Agent Services Site 2026

Date: 2026-08-30
Status: Accepted for production promotion after gated validation.

## Context

The previous homepage was primarily a public/legal reference. Jadel Tech RD now has a broader portfolio of agentic, media, analytics, integration, governance, revenue-research and quantitative-research repositories. The website needs to explain those capabilities without representing experimental or gated systems as production-ready.

## Decision

1. Keep GitHub Pages as the static origin and Cloudflare as the public DNS/TLS edge.
2. Preserve the existing public legal routes: `?view=privacy`, `?view=terms`, and `?view=data-deletion`.
3. Convert only the home experience into a commercial Agentic Systems Studio.
4. Use semantic HTML generated by dependency-free JavaScript plus CSS; no frontend framework or third-party browser runtime dependency is introduced.
5. Productize capabilities into a visible catalog with three maturity labels: service/implementable, supervised pilot, and research-only.
6. Add service filters, an interactive scope configurator, transparent launch pricing, methodology, governance controls, FAQ and structured data.
7. Never publish fabricated customers, logos, ROI, uptime, production status, profitability or autonomous capabilities.
8. Keep sensitive/external actions behind least-privilege permissions, deterministic policy gates and human approval when appropriate.
9. Add server-visible SEO metadata, canonical/Open Graph/Twitter metadata, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`.

## Market benchmark

Reviewed current public pricing and product-positioning patterns from Lindy, Relevance AI, Sintra, Beam AI, SmythOS, Stack AI, Salesforce Agentforce and CrewAI.

Observed market patterns:
- self-service agent software starts from free or tens of USD per month and scales into hundreds/month;
- usage/action/conversation pricing is common for variable workloads;
- managed implementation and enterprise agent programs range from four to five figures;
- high-converting pages make the product visible, explain outcomes, expose pricing or pricing logic, use interactive demos/configurators, and establish security/trust before enterprise CTAs.

Detailed source URLs and current public prices are recorded in `docs/MARKET-BENCHMARK-AI-AGENT-SERVICES-2026.md`.

## Launch pricing policy

Prices on the commercial site are launch estimates, not guaranteed quotes. Final scope is expected to vary roughly ±25% based on integrations, volume, security/compliance and operating requirements. Third-party model/API/SaaS usage, taxes and extraordinary compliance work are excluded unless explicitly quoted.

Top-level launch anchors:
- Agentic Blueprint: from US$250 one-time.
- Business Agent: from US$1,500 setup.
- Agentic System: from US$4,500 setup.
- Managed Agent Platform: custom, from US$10,000.
