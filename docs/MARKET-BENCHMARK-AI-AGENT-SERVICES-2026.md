## Market segmentation

### 1. Self-service agent software

Typical public entry point: approximately $0–$500/month.

Customer buys access to a builder/platform and remains responsible for much of the design, integration, testing, operations and governance.

### 2. Managed implementation

Observed public range: approximately $1,200–$18,000+/month or $10,000+ setup for custom implementations.

Customer buys engineering capacity, architecture, integration, security, testing, observability and operational support in addition to software.

### 3. Enterprise agent transformation

Often custom priced. Purchase criteria emphasize SSO/RBAC, audit logs, workload identity, private deployment, VPC/on-prem, policy controls, evaluations, onboarding and forward-deployed engineering.

## Jadel Tech RD positioning decision

Jadel Tech RD should not position itself as another low-cost self-service agent builder. The stronger position is:

> Agentic systems engineering and managed implementation with governance built in.

This allows pricing between commodity SaaS subscriptions and large enterprise consulting engagements while preserving transparent starter offers.

## Agent/service maturity policy

Public catalog claims must map to repository evidence:

- `available`: service can be sold as engineering/implementation work.
- `pilot`: architecture or connectors exist but production use still requires credential/platform/live-contract gates.
- `research`: research and technical advisory only; no live financial or autonomous-capital claims.

No service should be promoted to a stronger label solely because code exists. Promotion requires verifiable operational evidence.

## 2026 conversion/design patterns observed

High-performing agent/SaaS pages repeatedly use:

1. a single outcome-led hero statement;
2. visible product interaction or agent-console visualization;
3. role/use-case language rather than model jargon;
4. simple pricing entry points plus enterprise escalation;
5. security/governance controls before enterprise CTA;
6. explicit demos, workflows or templates;
7. social proof only when verifiable;
8. clear separation between evaluation/free usage and production deployment.

Jadel Tech RD deliberately avoids fabricated logos, testimonials, customer counts, ROI and production certifications.
