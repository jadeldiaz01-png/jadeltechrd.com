# Quant research separation of duties

Quantitative research is isolated from customer/commercial intake and from any live capital authority.

Stages are RESEARCH -> BACKTEST -> WALK_FORWARD/OFFLINE_EVAL -> PAPER -> TESTNET -> EVIDENCE_COMPLETE -> LIVE_PILOT_HUMAN_REVIEW. Promotion requires point-in-time data, leakage checks, realistic fees/slippage, regime/stress analysis, reproducibility, execution-quality evidence, risk limits, reconciliation and explicit human approval.

No model, optimizer or agent can self-promote a strategy to live trading. Statistical significance without implementation/execution evidence is insufficient. Expected return, backtest profit or paper PnL is not settled cash and must not be represented as guaranteed profitability.

Live-market credentials, if ever provisioned, are separated from research identities and use the minimum permissions required. Withdrawal authority is excluded unless a separate explicitly approved business need exists.

Commercial website services may sell research, engineering, risk systems and testnet readiness, but must not claim a profitable autonomous bot without current verified evidence.
