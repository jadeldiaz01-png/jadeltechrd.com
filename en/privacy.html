Privacy Policy

Source policy last updated: September 20, 2026.

This is an English translation of Jadel Tech RD's Spanish privacy policy. The Spanish source is available here.

This policy describes how Jadel Tech RD (“Jadel Tech RD,” “we,” or “our”) may collect, use, retain, and protect information when a person uses an application, service, or integration that refers to this site.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Jadel Tech RD digital services that expressly link to this page. Third-party services, including social networks, identity providers, cloud providers, and other external platforms, are also governed by their own policies.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the functions a user chooses to use, we may process:

  • Account and profile information the user provides directly.
  • Technical identifiers needed to authenticate sessions and operate integrations.
  • Information an external platform allows us to receive after the user expressly authorizes it.
  • Operational and diagnostic data, such as error records, security activity, and technical metrics.
  • Content the user submits to the application for a requested function.
  • Limited payment data or receipts submitted by the user; this site does not collect or store full card numbers.

We seek to limit collection to what is necessary to provide the requested functionality and meet security, abuse-prevention, and service-operation needs.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve application functions.
  • Authenticate users and maintain session security.
  • Perform actions or integrations the user requests and authorizes.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, technical failures, security incidents, and unauthorized use.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.
  • Respond to support, privacy, correction, or deletion requests.

4. Optional analytics

Jadel Tech RD may use Google Analytics 4 to measure site visits, navigation, and performance only when a visitor consents to analytics. Advertising storage and use remain disabled. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, form notes, free-text company information, payment identifiers, tokens, or secrets to Analytics.

The preference may later be changed using the “Analytics preferences” control. Declining analytics does not prevent use of essential site functions.

5. Third-party platform integrations

When a function needs to connect to an external platform, the application will use only permissions that have been granted and are necessary for the requested function. Authorization granted to a platform may be revoked using controls provided by that platform.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with providers that help us operate infrastructure, security, hosting, storage, technical analytics, or support, only when needed to provide the service and subject to appropriate controls. We may also disclose information where a valid legal obligation exists or disclosure is needed to protect rights, safety, or service integrity.

7. Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security records, meet applicable obligations, and resolve disputes. Specific periods may vary by data type and function.

8. Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security, so we review and improve controls as risks evolve.

9. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on the jurisdiction, a person may have rights to access, correct, object to, restrict, port, or delete certain personal information. To make a request, use the support or contact channel shown in the application or service that directed you to this page. We may request reasonable information to verify identity and prevent fraudulent requests.

9. Data deletion

A user may request deletion of data associated with their use of the service through the application's support or contact channel. Where appropriate, we will delete or anonymize data we are not required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution reasons.

10. Children

The services are not intentionally designed to collect children's data where applicable law requires parent or guardian authorization. If we learn that a child's data was improperly received, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. International transfers

Some infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than the user's country. Where applicable, we will use reasonable safeguards and measures for those transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, controls, or applicable requirements. We will publish the current version at this address and update the date shown at the start.

13. Contact

For privacy, support, or data-deletion questions: darklife_jadel@hotmail.com.
